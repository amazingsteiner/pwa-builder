DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Bold - bundled fonts
================================================
DejaVu fonts are based on Bitstream Vera and are released under a
permissive free license (Bitstream Vera License + Public Domain additions),
which explicitly permits bundling, embedding, and redistribution with
software, including modification. See https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
for the full license text. No attribution restriction prevents bundling.
